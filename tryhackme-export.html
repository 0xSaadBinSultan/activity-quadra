Update your TryHackMe graph

  1. Drag Export TryHackMe activity onto your browser’s bookmarks bar.
  2. Open your public tryhackme.com/p/USERNAME profile and wait for its yearly activity graph.
  3. Click that bookmark to download a JSON snapshot.
  4. Transfer the JSON to your phone. In Activity Trio, choose Import snapshot on the TryHackMe card and select it.

The helper reads the visible public yearly graph only. It does not read login cookies or send data anywhere.
